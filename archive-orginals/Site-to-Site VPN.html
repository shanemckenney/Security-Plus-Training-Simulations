Site-to-Site VPN Configuration

In this task, you are responsible for setting up a site-to-site VPN to securely connect two remote networks. The configuration must use the most secure encryption algorithms available to ensure confidentiality and integrity of the data. All traffic between the two sites should be fully encrypted over the VPN tunnel. Additionally, authentication between the VPN concentrators must be established using a shared secret password to ensure that only trusted devices can form the connection.
